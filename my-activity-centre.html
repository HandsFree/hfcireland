My Activity Centre

Developed by Hands Free Computing Ireland Ltd

My Activity Centre is an application for children with a neurodiversity, giving them access to a range of fun activities for entertainment, learning and development.

Resources can be selected through the home screen and relevant categories, which include Colouring, Puzzles, Games and Crafts. Once opened they can be printed out or used within the application with drawing and editing tools.

New resources can be added to the categories and will sync and download when the sync button is pressed. Access to online gaming and activity portals is also available through the home screen for even more entertainment and accessible games.

All games can run on multiple platforms including Microsoft Windows, Mac, Android and iOS.

Colouring

Open colouring resources in the app and use drawing tools, or print them out for offline use.

Puzzles

Choose from a range of puzzle activities designed to support learning, focus and enjoyment.

Games

Play accessible games in the app and through linked online gaming and activity portals.

Crafts

Explore craft resources that can be completed on screen or printed for hands-on making.

How it works

  • Open the home screen and choose a category: Colouring, Puzzles, Games or Crafts.
  • Use resources in the application with drawing and editing tools, or print them out.
  • Press sync to download new resources added to each category.
  • Use the home screen to reach additional online gaming and activity portals.

System requirements

  • 1 GHz or faster processor or SoC processor
  • 1 GB RAM for 32-bit or 2 GB RAM for 64-bit
  • 1 GB of available hard disk space
  • DirectX 9 or later with WDDM 1.0 driver graphics card
  • Noise cancelling microphone
  • Sound card capable of supporting 22 kHz 16-bit recording
  • An internet connection is required for activation and digital downloads

Google account and verification

My Activity Centre may use Google account sign-in to authenticate a parent, guardian or authorised user, activate the application, and manage access. Please read our public Privacy Policy and Terms of Service before using the application.
